## Data safety form

Shelfie collects nothing, which makes this the short version. Answer exactly:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | *(not asked once you answer No above)* |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **No** — and this is correct, see note |
| Does your app contain ads? | **No** |

**Data types — leave every category unticked.** Location, Personal info, Financial
info, Health, Messages, Photos and videos, Files and docs, App activity, Device IDs:
none of them.

### The one that looks wrong but is right

**Photos and videos → do not tick.**

Play's definition of "collect" is transmitting data off the device, or sharing it with
a third party. Shelfie reads your images entirely on-device and transmits nothing, so
there is no collection to declare. Ticking it would state that images are sent
somewhere, which is false and would trigger questions you cannot answer truthfully.

### The deletion question

Answer **No**, and if asked to elaborate: there is no server-side copy to delete,
because nothing is ever transmitted. Users can delete everything locally by clearing
app storage or uninstalling, and Settings offers a full data export. That is the
honest answer and reviewers accept it.

### If ads are ever added

Every answer above changes. AdMob collects an advertising ID and device information,
so *Device or other IDs* becomes ticked, *Contains ads* becomes Yes, `INTERNET` is
declared, and the privacy policy needs a section naming the ad network. Do not add ads
without updating all three of: this form, the policy page, and the in-app copy.

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC)

Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other**

Answer **No** to all of:

- Violence, blood, or gore of any kind
- Sexual or suggestive content, nudity
- Profanity or crude humour
- References to drugs, alcohol or tobacco
- Gambling, simulated gambling, or contests
- Horror or fear themes
- Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with each other?
- Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users?
- Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? → **Yes**

That last one is the only Yes: the one-time unlock is a digital purchase.

Expected result: **Everyone / 3+** in every territory.

**Note on the interaction question.** Answer No. Shelfie's share action hands a
screenshot to whatever app the user picks via Android's own share sheet; it has no
user-to-user features, accounts, or content exchange of its own. Answering Yes would
pull in social-app requirements that do not apply.

## Screenshots

Play needs at least 2 phone screenshots; 4–6 is better. Capture on a real device at
its native resolution.

In priority order, because the first two are what most people actually see:

1. **The shelf, full of real screenshots with category badges.** This is the "oh, I
   see what it does" image.
2. **A search in progress**, with results and the matched words highlighted. This is
   the core promise being demonstrated.
3. **The Find tab**, showing folders and categories.
4. **Where to look**, with WhatsApp and Telegram visible in the folder list.
5. **A detail sheet**, showing extracted text and the Copy action.
6. **Cleanup**, showing duplicates found.

Two things to get right before capturing:

- **Use a device with a genuinely full library.** A shelf with four screenshots on it
  looks like an unfinished app.
- **Check every screenshot for your own private data.** These are public forever.
  Real OTPs, bank balances, addresses and phone numbers are very easy to publish by
  accident here — this app's whole subject matter is sensitive material.
